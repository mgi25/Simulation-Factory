# Reference breakdown — `docs/references/neon_marble_machine_concept.png`

What the target actually is, part by part, so V2 can be measured against it
rather than against a memory of it.

## A. Silhouette

A **tapering vertical tower**, widest at the base finale and narrowest at the
start cap. Read as a stack of *discs* threaded on a dark core: three circular
plan-view modules (bowl, collector, finish) alternate with rectangular ones
(start, split deck). The track is the only thing that breaks the cylinder — it
swings well outside the tower's radius and comes back, and those excursions are
what stop the silhouette reading as a column. Roughly **1 : 3.5** width to
height, with the outline never straight for more than one module.

## B. Module proportions

The bowl is the widest single element and about **1.9×** the start's width. Each
module is **short** relative to its diameter — a disc, not a drum. Vertical gaps
between modules are ~0.6–1.0 module heights and are never empty: the track,
a chute or a bracket occupies them. Nothing in the frame is more than about two
module-widths from something else.

## C. Track shape language

A **fat, moulded ribbon**, not a road. Its width is roughly 2 marble diameters
of clear channel inside a shell about 1.5× that wide. Cross-section is a
**U-channel with rolled lips**, a light-silver running floor, and a visibly
**thick sculpted underside** — you can see the belly of the track from below at
every point. It banks into its turns. It carries a continuous glowing edge line
on the outboard lip, and periodic transverse joints. Curvature is continuous:
no straight segment meets an arc.

## D. Support shape language

**Dark graphite architecture, behind the action.** Not scaffolding: chunky
rectangular pylons with panel breaks, plus solid ring collars where a module
lands on them. Where a slender member appears it is a *pair* of angled struts
under a track, never a lattice. The supports are perhaps 40% of the frame's
dark area and 0% of its focus — they never cross a running surface from the
hero angle.

## E. Material hierarchy

Four surfaces, in order of frame area:
1. **Warm pearl-white moulded shell** — module bodies, track outer shell.
2. **Dark blue-graphite** — structure, chassis, undersides. Never black.
3. **Light silver** — running surfaces, rims, trim.
4. **Cyan-tinted clear acrylic** — bowl wall, guard walls, sign faces.
Metal is small and deliberate: gold hardware at collars and the finale, chrome
beads on rims. Accent light is **emissive line work**, not glowing paint — thin
strips in recesses, so the shell stays a shell.

## F. Environment

A **cliff gorge at dusk**. Rock walls left and right in two depth layers, warm
lit structures embedded in them, a valley of haze below, deep blue sky above
with a warm band at the horizon. The machine is *lit by* this world — cool from
above, warm from below and behind. Critically the background is only ~3 stops
under the subject and still resolves as shapes; it is separated by **atmosphere,
not by darkness**.

## G. Lighting

A soft cool key from upper-left, a strong cool rim from behind, a warm bounce
from the valley, and **practical lights that belong to the machine** (the ring
under the bowl, the sign, the finale's gold floods). Deep readable shadows under
every disc. Highlights roll off — the white shell is never clipped, it keeps
its curvature shading everywhere.

## H. Camera

A **long lens, low-to-mid three-quarter view**: roughly 16–22° elevation,
~30–40° azimuth off the module's facing, 30–35° FOV. The tower fills the full
height of a portrait frame with a margin of environment on each side. Almost no
convergence on verticals — the compression is what makes it read as a product
photograph rather than a game screenshot.

## I. Detail hierarchy

- **Large**: the disc-on-core silhouette, the track's excursions.
- **Medium** (the layer that carries it): rims, collars, guard frames, cradle
  arms, housings, bracket plates, panel breaks. Every module has 4–8 of these.
- **Small**: bolt rings, seams, light strips, small fascia panels. Regular and
  banded, never scattered — they read as texture, not as objects.

## What V2 must fix relative to the prior lab build

1. The support tower was a **lattice of thin rods** that crossed the modules —
   replace with two chunky pylons plus large brackets, all behind the action.
2. The background was **black** — replace with the cliff gorge.
3. The track read as a **flat white ribbon** — give it a real section, banking,
   a sculpted keel and a controlled highlight instead of a clipped stripe.
4. The start was a **platform with rails** — make it a moulded pod.
5. The bowl was a **cream plate** — make it a transparent shell over a silver
   dish, on a visible cradle, with marbles at a readable scale.
