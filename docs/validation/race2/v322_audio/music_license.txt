2. WHERE THE MUSIC COMES FROM
-----------------------------

Every sample of the bed is computed at render time by:

    audio/asmr.py          music_score()      the arrangement
                           music_plan()       the tempo and section map
                           music_tempo()      BPM derived from the race
                           _pluck()           one plucked note
                           CHORDS             the progression, as frequencies
                           _LAYER_OFFSETS     the per-part timing offsets

    audio/synthesis.py     tone()             one enveloped sine
                           noise_burst()      enveloped, band-limited noise
                           Noise              a seeded linear-congruential PRNG
                           envelope(), low_pass(), high_pass(), add_into()

`audio/synthesis.py` predates this pass and is itself original project code: it
contains oscillators, envelopes, one-pole filters and a seeded PRNG, and nothing
else. It reads no files. There is no audio data on disk anywhere in `audio/`.

The bed can therefore be regenerated from source at any time, and its provenance
is the git history of this repository.


3. WHAT THE MUSIC IS

    Key .................. A natural minor
    Progression .......... i - VI - III - VII (Am - F - C - G), four bars
    Roots ................ 220.00, 174.61, 261.63, 196.00 Hz
    Tempo ................ 136.565 BPM
    Metre ................ 4/4
    Length ............... 19.1667 s (10.9 bars)
    Parts ................ pulse (filtered noise), bass (sine + octave + sub),
                           figure (three-partial pluck), counter (pluck),
                           resolution (five-partial chord on the crossing)

The tempo is NOT a chosen number. It is computed from the race:

    music_tempo(crossing) = 9 bars * 4 beats * 60 / 15.81667 s = 136.565 BPM

so that the winner's crossing lands exactly on the downbeat of bar 9. Every
other section boundary follows from that. The arrangement is a function of the
replay, which is itself a deterministic product of seed 8.


4. ORIGINALITY

The progression i-VI-III-VII is a chord sequence. Chord progressions are not
copyrightable subject matter in any jurisdiction this project publishes in;
what is protected is a particular expression of one, and the expression here is
1400 lines of arithmetic in this repository with no reference to any existing
recording or score. The melodic figure is the root, fifth and octave of the
current chord played as three eighth notes - an arpeggio of the chord itself.

No melody, bassline, rhythm, arrangement or production element has been
transcribed, sampled, interpolated or modelled from any third-party work.


5. DETERMINISM AND REPRODUCIBILITY

The bed is a pure function of (replay seed, profile). It is seeded through
`audio.synthesis.stable_seed("v322-music", 8)`, uses no wall clock and no
unseeded randomness, and is covered by:

    tests/test_race2_v322_audio.py::test_the_same_profile_builds_the_same_audio_twice
    tests/test_race2_v322_audio.py::test_the_tempo_is_the_races_own

Rebuilding from this commit reproduces the delivered WAVs sample for sample.


6. THE REST OF THE SOUNDTRACK

The same applies to every non-musical sound in the mix. The rolling layer, the
rail ticks, the marble-to-marble clicks, the track contacts, the five mechanism
voices, the points ticks, the arrival cues and the room tone are all synthesised
by `audio/asmr.py` from `audio/synthesis.py` primitives. There is no field
recording and no sample library in the production.

The CONTROL mix carried in this comparison is `audio/marble.py`, which is the
same situation and was delivered with V19 through V32.1.
