So it is windowed. leg1 would move 20.78 degrees for a pocket of 0.0309,
because the constraint forbids EITHER edge rising - which also forbids
winding *on* faster than the drop pays for, and leg1 banks to 28 degrees.
The rule is stronger than 'no pocket': it is 'no point on the section ever
rises'. Inside leg2's window the roll is coming off throughout, so the two
coincide there; applied globally they do not.

Every bank extreme is preserved in every case, which is the whole reason
this is available where bank, radius and slope are not.
