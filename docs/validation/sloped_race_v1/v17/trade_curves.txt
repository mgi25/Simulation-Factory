The dynamic start equaliser's two trade curves, both measured on 48 seeds
(384 racers) of the isolated start lab with `--start-kind rotor --candidate
rotor-seeded`, everything else held.

"delivered" is 100% minus escapes minus racers still in the chamber when the
trial ended. "held%" is that second term - the fraction the mechanism never
released - which only became visible after `StartTrial._stall` was taught that
a marble which has never touched a run is still stuck. Before that fix both
this and V1.5's converging funnel reported `lost 0, stuck 0` while delivering
zero racers.

Not monotone, and 15 degrees is the tell: a floor steep enough to deliver the
field is a floor that packs it against the closed outlet while it is being
mixed, where the paddles cannot get between the marbles. Throughput improves
and the centre-versus-rank correlation climbs to +0.64 - the shipped taper's
own bias, recreated.

Monotone in the wrong direction: every unit of sweep that buys throughput
costs slot fairness. Delivering the field requires a rule that reads where each
marble is, and the field's positions still carry what the mixing left of the
bay.
